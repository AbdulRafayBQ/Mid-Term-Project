## 📌 About the Project

**Kaam Krwao** is a modern service marketplace web application designed to connect customers with skilled service professionals (Ustaads) for their everyday needs.

Customers can post their requirements, provide job details, select location and urgency, upload photos or videos, and find a suitable Ustaad for their work.

The core idea, concept, problem-solving approach, and overall product direction of **Kaam Krwao** were developed by **Abdul Rafay**, while **Atisamul Haq** contributed to the 3D UI and visual design of the project.

---

## ✨ Main Features
- 🤖 **AI-Powered Assistant** — Helps users understand their requirements and find suitable services (Using JS).
- 📝 **Job Posting** — Customers can post their service requirements.
- 👤 **Ustaad Profiles** — Professionals can showcase their skills and experience.
- ✅ **Ustaad Verification** — Verification flow for service professionals.
- 🛠️ **Service Categories** — Different categories for common services.
- ⭐ **Ratings & Reviews** — Customers can rate and review Ustaads.
- 📍 **Location & Urgency** — Customers can specify job location and urgency.
- 📷 **Photo Uploads** — Customers can attach images related to their job.
- 🎥 **Video Uploads** — Customers can share videos explaining the work.
- 💬 **Chat Interface** — Communication interface between customers and Ustaads.
- 📊 **Dashboards** — Separate dashboard experiences for customers and Ustaads.
- 🔔 **Notifications** — Interactive notifications and toast messages.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** — Optimized for mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
